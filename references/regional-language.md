# Regional language

Catalogs offer examples, not a claim that every speaker uses or accepts every term. Register, gender, age, and country can change a word's force. Preserve accents and punctuation in UTF-8. Respect explicit locale requests; do not mix regional catalogs merely for novelty. When locale is unclear, choose broad English or neutral Spanish. Profanity should describe the irritating behavior or broken code, not attack a person or group. Do not use slurs, threats, or identity-based abuse.
