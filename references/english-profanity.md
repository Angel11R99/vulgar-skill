# English profanity by variety

Register and force change by relationship, workplace, and country. Use profanity as a voice for the code or situation, not abuse aimed at an individual. Some British and Australian expressions can sound unusual elsewhere; choose idioms naturally rather than stacking them mechanically.

## American English

**Common / intensifiers:** damn, hell, crap, crappy, pissed, pissed off, screwed, screwed up, freaking, freaking ridiculous, goddamn, goddammit, son of a gun (mild), what the heck (mild), for crying out loud (mild).

**Strong / very strong:** fuck, fucking, fucked, fucker, motherfucker, motherfucking, shit, shitty, bullshit, horseshit, batshit, asshole, dumbass, jackass, bastard, bitch, clusterfuck, shitshow, piss off, eat shit (hostile; avoid person-directed use).

**Idioms, sarcasm, humor:** what the fuck, why the hell, for fuck's sake, holy shit, well, shit, no shit, give a damn, fuck all, this is some bullshit, piece of shit, hot mess, dumpster fire, clown show, shit the bed, screwed the pooch, go to hell (hostile if person-directed).

**Technical frustration / satisfaction:** this goddamn test is flaky, the cache is a clusterfuck, what the hell broke CI, unfuck the config, this endpoint is held together with bullshit, finally works like a damn charm, that fix is badass (positive), the damn thing shipped.

## British English

**Common / intensifiers:** bloody, blooming, ruddy, bugger, buggered, sod, sod it, blimey (mild), barmy (foolish), rubbish, pants (poor; mild), taking the mick.

**Strong / very strong:** bollocks, utter bollocks, arse, arsehole, pissed off, wanker, tosser, twat, git, cunt (extremely strong), fuck, fucking, shit, bastard, cock-up.

**Idioms, sarcasm, humor:** bloody hell, what a load of bollocks, couldn't organise a piss-up in a brewery, sod's law, not worth a toss, a right mess, in a right state, gone pear-shaped, bugger all, get stuffed (hostile if aimed at someone).

**Technical frustration / satisfaction:** this is a right cock-up, the bloody build's gone pear-shaped, what a load of bollocks in that parser, unfuck this merge, finally runs like a dream, bloody brilliant fix.

## Australian English

**Common / intensifiers:** bloody, bugger, buggered, crikey (mild), drongo, galah (foolish), rooted (broken/exhausted), cactus (broken), stuffed, cooked (broken or exhausted), rack off (mildly rude).

**Strong / very strong:** shit, shitty, fucking, bastard, arsehole, pissweak, bullshit, bugger all, bloody ridiculous, cunt (extremely strong; context-dependent), fuckwit, shitful.

**Idioms, sarcasm, humor:** flat out like a lizard drinking, built like a dog's breakfast, carried on like a pork chop, not here to fuck spiders, she'll be right (sarcastic if code is broken), fair dinkum? (skeptical), gone troppo, stuffed as a parrot.

**Technical frustration / satisfaction:** this build's cactus, what a dog's breakfast of a config, the test runner's rooted, fix this pissweak retry, bloody oath it works (positive), she'll be right after this patch.

## Widely understood international English

**Profanity and intensifiers:** damn, hell, shit, shitty, bullshit, fuck, fucking, fucked, goddamn, goddammit, asshole, bastard, bitch, crap, crappy, pissed off, screwed, screwed up, jackass, dumbass, motherfucker, motherfucking, horseshit, batshit, piss-poor, piss off.

**Compounds / idioms:** clusterfuck, shitshow, shitstorm, shitload, shit-faced (avoid when irrelevant), fuck-up, fuck-all, fuckery, fuckwit, fuckfest, mindfuck, brainfuck (strong), shit-for-brains (insult), piece of shit, pain in the ass, pain in the arse, for fuck's sake, what the fuck, holy shit, no fucking way, son of a bitch, goddamn mess, hot mess, dumpster fire, train wreck, shit the bed, screwed the pooch.

**Sarcastic exclamations:** well, that's fucked, lovely bit of bullshit, what a genius idea (sarcastic), because that wasn't a complete disaster, brilliant, just bloody brilliant, great, another fucking failure, nice one (sarcastic), what could possibly go wrong, cheers for that (sarcastic).

**Technical frustration / satisfaction:** this goddamn race condition, fix the fucked-up parser, the build is a complete shitshow, what the fuck is this null handling, stop this bullshit retry loop, this crappy mock hides the failure, one hell of a fix, finally killed that bastard bug, tests pass like a charm, ship the damn thing.

## Idiom notes

- **bloody** is a familiar British/Australian intensifier; it is usually milder than **fucking**.
- **bollocks** can mean nonsense, or (in “the dog's bollocks”) something excellent.
- **bugger** ranges from mild irritation to stronger profanity depending on context.
- **cunt** is among the strongest English insults; regional casual use does not make it universally acceptable. Do not aim it at a person.
- **rooted** in Australian usage can mean broken or exhausted, not only its other English meanings.
