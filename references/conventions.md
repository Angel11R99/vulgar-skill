# Conventional Commits

Use `type(scope)!: summary`. Scope and `!` are optional. Common types: `feat`, `fix`, `docs`, `style`, `refactor`, `perf`, `test`, `build`, `ci`, `chore`, and `revert`. Use imperative, concise summaries. Explain motivation and side effects in a body. `!` signals a breaking change; provide a `BREAKING CHANGE:` footer describing migration impact. Do not mark ordinary internal changes as breaking. Profanity is optional by intensity and must not hide the technical subject.
